Live from PostgreSQL, read-only. Consumption is gold.energy_consumption_validated.validated_kwh_consumption — the same column your Power BI report sums, so the totals match by construction, not by coincidence. Electrical signals come from gold.energy_raw at the raw meter cadence — measured median 244 s, about 4.07 min, so roughly 354 readings per meter-day — joined to gold.meter, gold.zone and gold.facility.
Status bands are your own silver.classify_*_v2 functions: Vn 215–245 acceptable, VL 380–420, PF 0.80–1.00, Hz 59.3–60.5, current against each meter's CB rating. overall_status is worst_status across Vn1-3, VL1-3, Hz, PF1-3, PFa and kWh — it deliberately excludes the three current channels, so per-phase current status is shown separately.
